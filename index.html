About Me

I am Chaity Adhikary, an education graduate from the Institute of Education and Research (IER), University of Dhaka, with a background in Social Science Education.

My academic interests focus on female education, school dropout, out-of-school children and the educational experiences of forcibly displaced populations, particularly the Rohingya/FDMN community.

I am interested in understanding educational inequality and exploring inclusive, equitable and context-sensitive approaches that can create meaningful learning opportunities for underserved communities.

“Small steps can create meaningful changes.”
